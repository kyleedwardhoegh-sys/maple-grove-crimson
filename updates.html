Welcome to Maple Grove Crimson — 5th Grade Tackle!

Hi 5th Grade Tackle families,

Welcome to the season! I'm Kyle Hoegh, your head coach, and I'm genuinely excited for this group.

Meet Your Coaches

Alongside me, Dom Barber is our co-head coach, and Thomas Barber and Pat Truax are our assistant coaches. You'll be hearing from all of us this season.

We'd Love a Team Manager

Every team needs one parent volunteer to serve as Team Manager, helping coordinate weekly communication. It's a great way to stay close to the team — and it covers your DIBS volunteer requirement for the season. If you're interested, just reply to this email or grab me at practice.

My Coaching Philosophy

Winning and having fun aren't our goals — they're the result of our real goal: improving every day at playing the game of football with discipline, skill, heart, effort, and for each other. If we do that, not only will we win and have fun, but we'll lay the foundation to love football and win championships. And the discipline, work ethic, and character we build here won't stop at the sideline — they'll help these boys succeed in life and in whatever career they choose.

That's the approach I bring as head coach — you'll get to know each of our coaches' own styles as the season goes on. Our shared non-negotiables are on the team site — please go over them with your son before Monday. The ones we'll be leaning on hardest early: full effort every rep, no walking between drills, and eyes on the coach the moment we're talking.

First Practice: Monday, August 17, 5:30–7:00 PM

Fernbrook Athletic Fields — 14401 99th Ave N, Maple Grove, MN 55369. We're on Field 1, the farthest field back, in the back left corner once you enter — there's no sign on-site, but you'll see "Hoegh" labeled on the field map on the Location page.

What to bring: shorts & t-shirt, athletic cleats, helmet, mouth guard (required — any color is fine, but no chains, charms, or attachments; league rules make those illegal), and a water bottle. Get him drinking water through the day, not just right before practice.

Gear timeline, first two weeks: Practices 1–2 (Mon/Tue) — helmet only, no contact. Practices 3–4 (Wed/Thu) — add shoulder pads, still no contact. Practice 5 on (week of Aug 24) — add black padded football pants; full pads, full contact begins.

$100 check due next week. Starting the week of August 24th, every player needs to bring a $100 check made out to MGYFA — this covers your DIBS requirement and helmet deposit, and it's required even if you've already done DIBS or own your own helmet. Check only, no cash or Venmo — hand it to our Team Manager.

Crossbar: RSVP for every practice and game. Please mark your son's availability in Crossbar for every practice and game — that's how I'll know who to expect, and it's the fastest way to flag a late arrival or absence. Make sure your notifications are on too, since Crossbar Chat and email are how I'll be reaching the team.

One heads-up: traffic around the complex should be light for week one, but once Flag Football practices start the week of August 24th (Tue/Wed/Thu evenings), expect more cars — give yourself a few extra minutes for drop-off.

Can't wait to get started Monday — see you all on the field!
