Coaching Philosophy

Winning and having fun are not our goals — they're the result of our real goal: improving every day at playing the game of football with discipline, skill, heart, effort, and for each other.

If we do that, not only will we win and have fun, but we'll lay the foundation to love football and win championships. And the discipline, work ethic, and character we build here won't stop at the sideline — they'll help these boys succeed in life and in whatever career they choose.

— Coach Kyle

Non-Negotiables

  1. Triple Whistle. Anytime a coach does a triple whistle (short–short–long), immediately drop what you're doing and run to the coach for further instruction.
  2. No Walking. No walking between drills.
  3. Full Effort. Full effort at all times.
  4. Patience & Positivity. Patience and positivity when learning new skills.
  5. Build Each Other Up. Encouraging and building teammates up.
  6. Eyes on the Speaker. Full eye contact and silence whenever a coach is talking.
  7. On Time & Ready. Dressed and ready — cleats on and tied — at the exact start time of practice.
  8. Attendance. RSVP every practice and game in Crossbar so we know who to expect.
